Audio Converter

Turn MP4 into MP3, MP3 into OGG, or almost any audio or video file into the format you need. The conversion runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Quality

The first conversion downloads the converter once (about 31 MB), after that your browser keeps it. Big files need a fair amount of memory, so on a phone stick to smaller ones. Conversion is done by FFmpeg (GPL-2.0-or-later) compiled to WebAssembly with ffmpeg.wasm, whose source you can find there.
