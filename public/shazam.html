Song Recognition

Drop an audio clip and get the song: title, artist, album and links to streaming services. Same lookup as the bot's /shazam command, powered by AudD.

Your file is sent to AudD for matching and not stored on our side. Works best with a clear stretch of music — humming rarely counts. Up to 20 MB.
