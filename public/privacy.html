Privacy Policy

last updated: September 2026

Short version up front: visitors don't need accounts, there's no tracking network, and I don't sell data. The one place you hand over personal data is the support form. What does get processed technically is listed here — as precisely as possible, without unnecessary fine print.

01 Who's responsible

The person behind system98 is responsible for data processing on this site. Contact details are in the About section of the homepage.

02 What data is processed

Visiting the site and using the tools involves the following data:

  • Server logs: IP address, time of access, page requested, and browser info — technically necessary for running the site and protecting against abuse. Visitors don't have accounts, so this can't be permanently tied to a specific person.
  • Support tickets: if you write to support, your name, email address, chosen language and the messages of the conversation are stored so the team can answer and follow up. Only signed-in team members can read tickets; nobody else can, except through your personal ticket link. You can ask for a ticket to be deleted at any time.
  • Team accounts: people who answer tickets have accounts (email, username, hashed password and an optional profile). Team profiles are only public if the person chooses to make them so.
  • Uploaded files: images you upload (e.g. for the background remover) are only processed to produce the result, then not kept afterward.

03 Cookies

No tracking cookies are set for the site's core functions (using the tools, switching language/theme, sending a support ticket). Team members get one technically necessary session cookie after logging in, so the site knows they're signed in.

04 Sharing with third parties

Data isn't sold or shared for advertising purposes. What's involved is limited to:

  • Hosting provider: processes server logs as part of running the site.
  • Online counter: while a page is open, your browser sends a random ID (kept only in that browser tab, not linked to you, no cookie, no IP stored) about every 45 seconds so the footer can show how many pages are open right now. These entries are deleted after a few minutes.
  • Discord bot: when you use a command of the system98 bot, Discord sends us the command text and your Discord user ID. We use them to answer, apply a rate limit in memory and look the Roblox name or ID up on Roblox's public web APIs. For the /shazam command the attached audio file is sent to the AudD recognition service (audd.io) to identify the song; it is not stored by us. For the /qr command the QR image is generated on our server from the text you provide and not stored. Nothing else is stored.
  • Roblox user lookup: the username, ID or link you enter is looked up once by our server on Roblox's public web APIs and is not stored. Only public profile information is shown. Avatar images are loaded from Roblox's own servers, which see your IP address.
  • Roblox audio info: the ID or link you enter is looked up once by our server on Roblox's public web APIs and is not stored. Roblox thumbnails are loaded from Roblox's own servers, which see your IP address. Audio files you drop for analysis are processed inside your browser and never uploaded.
  • Image converter: images are converted inside your browser and are never uploaded to our server.
  • Audio converter: files are converted inside your browser and are never uploaded to our server. Your browser downloads the converter program (ffmpeg.wasm) from this site once.
  • Website status tool: the address you type in is requested once by our server to see whether the site answers. The address and the result are not stored; your watchlist lives only in your browser (localStorage).
  • Email provider: if ticket emails are switched on, your name, email address and the ticket text pass through the mail provider that delivers them.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google's servers to make the site look nice — this can send your IP address to Google.

05 Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, and restrict processing of your data, as well as data portability, where applicable. Since visitors don't have accounts, in practice this concerns server logs and any support tickets you've sent. For questions, just reach out via the About section. You can also file a complaint with a data protection authority at any time.

06 Changes to this policy

If something changes about the tools or the technical setup, this policy might change too. The current version always applies — the date above shows the last update.

Not a substitute for legal advice. This text is deliberately kept simple and covers the main points, but it doesn't replace an individual legal review under GDPR or other applicable law.
